## Overview

This project aims to provide a centralized digital platform for a barbershop, combining a customer-facing website with an appointment management system.

Customers can explore services, view barber profiles, check availability, and schedule appointments online. Business administrators can manage appointments, services, barber information, and operating schedules through a dedicated dashboard.

The objective is to develop a practical business solution that improves accessibility, streamlines appointment scheduling, and supports daily operations.

## Core Features

### Customer Website

- Responsive and user-friendly interface
- Business information and location
- Services and pricing
- Barber profiles and specialties
- Online appointment booking
- Date and time selection
- Booking confirmation and status

### Admin Dashboard

- Appointment overview and management
- Barber profile management
- Service and pricing management
- Barber schedules and availability
- Appointment status management
